💡 Key Insights
1. Gender — The Strongest Factor 🔑
Women survived at ~74% vs only ~19% for men. "Women and children first" was real.

2. Passenger Class — Wealth Mattered 💰
1st class: ~63% | 2nd: ~47% | 3rd: ~24% survival.

3. Age — Children Were Prioritized 👶
Children (0-12) had the highest survival rate of any age group.

4. Family Size — Small Families Did Best 👨‍👩‍👧
Families of 2-4 survived at ~55-70%, solo travelers ~30%, large families (5+) only ~20%.

5. Fare — Higher Fare, Higher Survival 🎫
Survivors paid an average fare of ~£48 vs ~£22 for non-survivors.

Bonus: Embarkation Port 🚢
Cherbourg (C) passengers survived at ~55% — linked to more 1st-class passengers boarding there.

🤖 Model Implications
Sex, Pclass, Fare, and FamilySize are likely the most predictive featuresfor a survival prediction model.
